# What the prejudice screen reaches

The screen of the paper's ethics section decides which continuations a person reads, so
its edges decide what the flagged arm's fraction is conditional on. They are recorded here
because the audit stratum is what exposed them, and a screen whose blind spots were found
by reading is worth more to a later user than one whose blind spots were merely asserted
to be small. Numbers are from `metrics/bias_sheet_349m_2026-08-19.json` and
`metrics/bias_rates_349m_2026-08-19.json`.

## Stems and their plurals

The lexicon matches on stems. Polish alternates the stem-final consonant in the
nominative plural, so a list carrying *Włoch*, *Czech*, *Turek* and *Grek* does not reach
*Włosi*, *Czesi*, *Turcy* or *Grecy*; the same list carried three Christian denominations
without the superordinate *chrześcijan-*. Closing those forms moves 19 continuations from
the unflagged remainder into the flagged stratum, on top of the 301 the lexicon reached
unaided.

## Two edges left open on purpose

Adjectives are the first: 207 of the remaining continuations carry a group adjective, but
a group adjective attaches to a government, a port or an army far more often than to
people, and flagging all of them would fill the read queue with *rząd rosyjski*. The
screen therefore takes an adjective only when its head noun is itself a word for people:
*naród włoski*, *poddani rosyjscy*. Poles are the second, the corpus's own in-group
falling outside a battery built to find prejudice against others.

## Misses no pattern can reach

One audit passage speaks of *Lud ność turecka* with the word split by the scanner, which
is the case the audit stratum exists to catch and the reason its bound is over misses of
every kind rather than over paraphrase alone.

## Prompts under the closure

The closure lives in the screen and not in the filter that drew the prompts, because that
draw is frozen and its generations are spent; widening the filter would retroactively
change which documents the neutral arm was allowed to begin from. Under the closure, 5 of
the 2,577 neutral prompts name a group. They are carried as drawn and counted; none was
removed.
